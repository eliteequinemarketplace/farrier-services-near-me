What to Look for in a Hunter Jumper Farrier

A farrier for hunter jumper horses needs specific expertise — the ability to balance feet correctly for the movement demands of hunter competition, familiarity with the range of shoes and modifications used in competitive hunter jumper sport, good communication with the veterinarians and trainers involved in a horse's care program, and the reliability to maintain consistent schedules for horses on an active show program. A great farrier is a trusted partner in a horse's athletic health and career.

Therapeutic Farriery for Competition Horses

Therapeutic or corrective farriery — working in conjunction with veterinarians to address specific soundness issues through shoeing modifications — is particularly important in the high-performance hunter jumper world. Horses competing intensively on hard footing, jumping demanding courses, and traveling frequently have specific hoof health needs that benefit from a farrier with therapeutic expertise.
